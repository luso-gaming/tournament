01

Eligibility

  • 16 years or older — Participation is restricted to players aged 16 and above, in line with BGMI's own age requirements. Players under 18 should have parental consent.
  • One account per user or team — multiple accounts are strictly prohibited.
  • Users must provide accurate and truthful information at registration.
  • Mobile devices only — emulators are strictly prohibited in all tournaments.
02

Verification

Identity verification using a valid government-issued ID may be required for certain platform features in the future, including prize withdrawals when sponsored tournaments are introduced. All data is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

03

Tournament System

  • Scoring is automatic and based on match performance — kills and placement points.
  • Live leaderboards update automatically after each tournament.
  • No manual score submission is required from players.
  • Room ID and Password are shared only with registered participants 20 minutes before match start.

05

Fair Play

🚫 Prohibited Software

No hacks, aimbots, speed hacks, wall hacks, or any third-party tools that provide an unfair advantage.

🚫 Emulators

Emulator usage is strictly prohibited. All players must compete on genuine mobile devices.

🚫 Account Sharing

Playing on behalf of another registered player or sharing accounts is not permitted.

🚫 Misconduct

Abusive language, harassment, hate speech, or unsportsmanlike behaviour results in immediate disqualification.

Violations may result in match disqualification, a 30-day ban, or permanent account termination. See our Punishments & Rules page for full details.

06

Prizes & Payments

Currently, all tournaments are free with no cash prizes. The following terms will apply when sponsored tournaments with prize pools are introduced:

  • Prizes will be distributed via UPI transfer to winners.
  • Winners must provide valid UPI details and PAN card for prize distribution.
  • TDS will be deducted as applicable under Indian Income Tax laws on prize winnings.
  • Luso Esports does not retain any profit from tournament fees — all fees (if any) cover prize pools and organizer operational costs.
07

Refund Policy

No payments are currently collected on this platform. A full refund policy will be published when paid features are introduced. In the event of a tournament cancellation by Luso Esports, any fees collected will be refunded in full within 7 working days.
