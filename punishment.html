Luso Esports – Punishment & Disciplinary Policy

This policy is designed to maintain fair play, discipline, and a competitive environment across all tournaments organized by Luso Esports Team.

1. Minor Violations

Examples include:

  • Late joining the match
  • Not following lobby instructions
  • Minor unintentional mistakes

Punishment:

  • Official warning
  • Match removal if required
  • Repeated offenses may lead to higher penalties

2. Moderate Violations

Examples include:

  • Sharing Room ID or Password
  • Ignoring official instructions
  • Using abusive or disrespectful language
  • Unsportsmanlike behavior

Punishment:

  • Immediate match disqualification
  • Temporary ban (7 to 30 days)
  • Removal from ongoing or upcoming tournaments

3. Major Violations

Examples include:

  • Cheating, hacking, or third-party tools
  • Teaming, ghosting, or exploiting game bugs
  • Account sharing or fake accounts
  • Threatening or attempting to manipulate admins

Punishment:

  • Immediate disqualification
  • Permanent ban from all Luso Esports tournaments
  • Blacklisting from future events

4. False Claims & Misuse

Examples include:

  • Submitting fake screenshots or recordings
  • False accusations against players or admins
  • Misuse of tournament systems or information

Punishment:

  • Disqualification from the current tournament
  • Temporary or permanent ban based on severity

5. Admin Authority

  • All decisions by the Luso Esports Team are final
  • Admins may review evidence before taking action
  • Punishments may vary based on intent and past behavior
By participating in any Luso Esports tournament, players automatically agree to follow this punishment policy along with all tournament rules and guidelines.
