This policy maintains fair gameplay, discipline, and a competitive environment across all tournaments organized by Luso Esports. By participating in any tournament, you automatically agree to this policy.

Minor Violation

Minor Violations

⚠️
Examples
  • Not joining the match on time
  • Not following room owner instructions
  • Minor unintentional mistakes during a match
Punishment
  • Official warning issued
  • Match removal if required
  • Repeated offenses escalate to higher penalties
02
Moderate Violation

Moderate Violations

🚫
Examples
  • Sharing Room ID or Password with outsiders
  • Deliberately ignoring official instructions
  • Using abusive or disrespectful language
  • Inappropriate or abusive profile picture
Punishment
  • Immediate match disqualification
  • Temporary ban — 7 to 30 days
  • Removal from ongoing or upcoming tournaments
03
Major Violation

Major Violations

💀
Examples
  • Cheating, hacking, or using third-party tools
  • Teaming, ghosting, or exploiting game bugs
  • Account sharing or use of fake accounts
  • Threatening or attempting to manipulate admins
  • Using emulators in mobile-only tournaments
Punishment
  • Immediate disqualification from the match
  • Permanent ban from all Luso Esports tournaments
  • Blacklisting from all future events
04
Misuse & False Claims

False Claims & Misuse

🕵️
Examples
  • Submitting fake screenshots or recordings as evidence
  • False accusations against players or admins
  • Misuse of tournament systems or information
Punishment
  • Disqualification from the current tournament
  • Temporary or permanent ban depending on severity
⚖️
Section 05

Admin Authority

All decisions made by the Luso Esports Team are final and binding.

Admins may review available evidence before taking action. Submit evidence within 30 minutes of match completion.

Punishments may vary based on intent, context, and past behaviour. First-time offenders may receive lighter penalties.

To dispute a decision, contact us at lusoesports.in@gmail.com or via the Grievance Redressal page.

Player Agreement

By participating in any Luso Esports tournament — free or sponsored — players automatically agree to follow this Punishment Policy along with all tournament rules and guidelines. Ignorance of this policy is not an accepted defence.
