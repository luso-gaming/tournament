01
Core

Tournament Rules

  1. All players must follow match schedules and instructions shared by Luso Esports.
  2. Room ID and password are confidential — never share with anyone outside the match.
  3. Use of hacks, cheats, emulators, VPNs, or any unfair advantage is strictly prohibited.
  4. Players must join matches on time. Late entry may lead to disqualification.
  5. Abusive behaviour, toxic chat, or misconduct towards any player or admin is not allowed.
  6. Each player or team is allowed only one account. Duplicate accounts will be banned.
  7. All decisions made by Luso Esports are final and binding.
📋

Participation in any tournament means you agree to all rules on this page.
