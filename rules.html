Tournament Rules & Guidelines

  1. All tournaments organized by Luso Esports Team are completely free to participate in.
  2. Players must strictly follow match schedules and instructions shared through official channels.
  3. Room IDs and passwords will be shared shortly before the match and must not be shared with anyone.
  4. Cheating, hacking, teaming, exploiting bugs, or any form of unfair gameplay is strictly prohibited.
  5. Players must join the room on time. Late joining may result in disqualification.
  6. Misbehavior, abusive language, or unsportsmanlike conduct will not be tolerated.
  7. All decisions made by the Luso Esports Team are final and binding.
  8. Failure to follow these rules may result in match removal or a permanent ban from future tournaments.
By participating in any Luso Esports tournament, players agree to follow all the rules listed above.
