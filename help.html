Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join a tournament? ▼
Go to the Join page, click Join Tournament on the active tournament card, and you will be registered. Make sure you are logged in with your registered account before joining.
Is the entry free? ▼
Yes, All the tournaments are free to enter.
How many teams are allowed per match? ▼
Each match usually allows up to 16 teams. Slots are limited, so register early to secure your spot. Once slots are full, registration closes automatically.
Where will the room ID be shared? ▼
Room details are shared before the match on the same tournament card you joined from. Check 10–15 minutes before match time. Do not leave the page.
What happens if I join late? ▼
Players must join the room on time. Late entry will not be allowed once the match has started. No exceptions will be made for late joiners.
Can I change my team after registration? ▼
Team changes are generally not allowed after registration. If you have an urgent issue, contact support on Instagram before the match starts.
What if my internet disconnects during the match? ▼
Luso Esports is not responsible for internet issues, server problems, or device failures. Ensure a stable connection before joining any match room.
Are cheats or hacks allowed? ▼
Absolutely not. Any unfair gameplay, cheating, or use of third-party apps will result in a permanent ban from all Luso Esports tournaments.
Can I report a cheater? ▼
Yes. Email us at lusoesports.in@gmail.com with your team name, match details, and any evidence such as screenshots or screen recordings. All reports are reviewed seriously.
My account was banned. Can I appeal? ▼
Yes. Send a ban appeal email to lusoesports.in@gmail.com with your registered email address and your reason for appeal. We review all appeals within 48 hours.
How are winners decided? ▼
Winners are decided based on kills and placement points according to the scoring rules published on each tournament card. The organizer's decision is final.
When will results be announced? ▼
Results are usually announced shortly after the match ends on our website leaderboard. For major events, results are also shared on our Instagram and WhatsApp channel.
Will there be prizes? ▼
Some tournaments include cash prizes. Prize details are always mentioned on the tournament card and our social media before registration opens. Prize payouts happen within 24 hours of results.
Is my data safe? ▼
Yes. Your data is only used for tournament operations and support purposes. We do not sell or share your data with third parties. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Do I need WhatsApp or Discord? ▼
WhatsApp is recommended for receiving updates on your support queries. You can also follow our WhatsApp channel for tournament announcements. No other apps are required.
