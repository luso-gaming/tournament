Origin Story

From WhatsApp Group to National Platform

Luso Esports didn't start with fancy servers or corporate backing. It began as a simple WhatsApp group on 10 November 2025, where a small group of serious BGMI players gathered to compete properly. Every Sunday, we hosted weekly tournaments with a real prize pool.

The format was simple but effective — 14–16 teams competed daily throughout the week to climb the leaderboard. Only the top 16 teams earned a spot in the high-stakes Sunday finals. This created intense daily competition and real stakes for every match.

From November 2025 to February 2026, we organized 14+ weekly tournaments plus additional development-phase events. What started as a weekend experiment quickly became a daily competitive hub for serious BGMI players.
