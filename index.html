How to Join BGMI Tournaments

Joining a BGMI tournament on Luso Esports is simple, fast, and completely free. Follow the steps below to participate in our upcoming tournaments.

1. Create an Account

Sign up on Luso Esports using your email or mobile number. Make sure your details are correct to avoid any issues during registration.

2. Choose a Tournament

Visit the Tournaments page and select the BGMI tournament you want to participate in.

3. Register for the Match

Fill in your BGMI in-game details such as Player ID, IGN, and team information (if applicable), then submit the registration form.

4. Join the Room

Room ID and password will be shared before the match via the website or official communication channels.

5. Play Fair & Compete

Join the match on time, follow the tournament rules, and play fair to secure your position on the leaderboard.

⚠️ Note: Any use of cheats, hacks, or fake information may lead to immediate disqualification or ban.
