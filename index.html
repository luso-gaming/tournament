How to Join BGMI Tournaments

Joining a BGMI tournament on Luso Esports is simple, fast, and completely free. Follow the steps below to participate in our upcoming tournaments.

1. Create an Account

Sign up on Luso Esports using your email.
Note: Remember the email you used to log in.

2. Fill your Details

Visit the My Account page and click on update to Fill your Team name and In-game name and click save. Now you can participate in the tournaments.
Note: You can change your details once every 14 days

3. Choose a Tournament

Visit the Tournaments page select the BGMI tournament which is active and you want to participate in.

4. Join the Room

When click on the join button make sure you are login and your team name and your in-game name is filled. Room ID / password and slot number will be shared 20 min before the match start via the website on the same card you joined.

5. Play Fair & Compete

Join the match on time, follow the tournament rules, and play fair to secure your position on the leaderboard.

Note: Any use of cheats, hacks, or fake information may lead to immediate disqualification or ban.
