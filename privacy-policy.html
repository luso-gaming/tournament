01

Information We Collect

Data We Collect
  • Email address (via Google OAuth)
  • Display name or full name (if provided)
  • Team name and in-game name (IGN)
  • Match results — kills, points, placements
  • Basic technical data — IP address, browser type, device type
  • Cookies and session data
We Never Collect
  • Passwords — we use Google OAuth only
  • Payment card or bank details
  • Aadhaar or government ID numbers
  • Location data or contacts
  • Any data from children under 16
02

How We Use Your Data

  • Manage tournament registrations, slots, and match scheduling.
  • Generate leaderboards, rankings, and match result pages.
  • Provide user support and resolve disputes.
  • Improve website performance, features, and user experience.
  • Send important platform notifications such as tournament updates and results.
  • Comply with applicable Indian laws and regulatory requirements.

We do not use your data for advertising profiling or sell it to third parties for marketing purposes.

  • Email address

04

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL). We apply the following security measures:

  • Row Level Security (RLS) policies on all database tables — users can only access their own data.
  • Google OAuth for authentication — we never store passwords.
  • HTTPS enforced on all pages — all data encrypted in transit.
  • No sensitive financial data is stored on our servers.

Report any security concerns to lusoesports.in@gmail.com immediately.

05

Age Requirement

Our platform is intended for users aged 16 years or older, in line with BGMI's own age requirements. Users under 18 should have parental consent before registering. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If we become aware that a user is under 16, their account will be removed immediately.

Your Rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

👁
Access

Request a copy of personal data we hold about you.

✏️
Correction

Update or correct inaccurate information in your profile.

🗑️
Deletion

Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data.

📤
Portability

Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact lusoesports.in@gmail.com. We will respond within 15 days.

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. Match result data (team name, IGN, scores) may be retained indefinitely for leaderboard and historical record purposes. Upon account deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days, except where retention is required by Indian law.
