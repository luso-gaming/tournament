Privacy Policy – Luso Esports

At Luso Esports (www.lusoesports.in), your privacy is our top priority. This comprehensive Privacy Policy explains exactly what personal data we collect from our nationwide BGMI player community, how we use it to power fair tournaments, and how we protect it under Indian IT Act 2000 and emerging data protection laws.

As a 100% Indian-owned platform founded by Sumit Vishwakarma from Sultanpur, Uttar Pradesh, we handle data responsibly for 20+ states worth of players who trust us with their tournament registrations, match results, and competitive journey.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only essential data needed to run professional BGMI tournaments:

Registration Data (Account Creation)

  • User Email Address – Primary communication channel
  • Full Name – Account verification and contact
  • Team Name – Tournament identification (change allowed every 14 days)
  • In-Game Name (IGN) – Match verification (change allowed every 14 days)

Tournament Performance Data

  • Match Results – Wins, losses, placements
  • Individual Scores – Kills, deaths, KD ratio
  • Points Earned – Leaderboard calculations
  • Matches Played – Weekly/seasonal statistics

Technical Data

  • Google Analytics – Page views, session duration, traffic sources
  • Cookies – Login sessions, preferences, security
  • IP Address – Security monitoring and fair play

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data powers everything that makes Luso Esports great:

  • Tournament Management: Team matching, bracket creation, qualification tracking
  • Leaderboard Generation: Weekly rankings based on team name, IGN, kills, points
  • Live Streaming: YouTube broadcasts with team performance displays
  • Cheat Investigation: Email requests for match proof when suspicious activity detected
  • Player Support: Ticket system responses, ban appeals, dispute resolution
  • Performance Analytics: Historical data for players to track progress across seasons
  • Website Improvement: Google Analytics insights for better user experience

3. Public Leaderboards & Transparency

Our public leaderboards display limited data for competitive transparency:

✅ Publicly Visible:

  • Team Name
  • In-Game Name (IGN)
  • Kills per match
  • Points earned
  • Match placements
  • Weekly/seasonal rank

🔒 Never Public:

  • Email addresses
  • Personal phone numbers
  • Full legal names
  • IP addresses
  • Login session data

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is protected in enterprise-grade infrastructure:

  • Database: Supabase (PostgreSQL) – Industry-standard security
  • Retention: Tournament data kept for multiple seasons for progress tracking
  • Access Control: Role-based permissions, only essential staff access
  • Encryption: Data at rest and in transit encrypted
  • Backups: Automated daily backups with 7-day retention

5. Age Requirements & Parental Consent

Luso Esports tournaments are for players aged 16+ only. We do not knowingly collect data from users under 16. Parents/guardians of players under 18 should monitor tournament participation.

We NEVER collect:

  • Bank account details
  • Credit/debit card information
  • Bank OTPs or transaction passwords
  • Government ID proofs

⚠️ SCAM WARNING: Luso Esports will never ask for banking details. Any individual requesting such information in our name is a fraud. We are not responsible for losses from sharing sensitive information.

6. Cookies & Login Sessions

We use minimal cookies for essential functionality:

  • Session Cookies: Keep you logged in (deleted on logout)
  • Preference Cookies: Remember leaderboard preferences
  • Analytics Cookies: Google Analytics (optional, can be disabled)

You can manage cookies via browser settings or our cookie consent banner.

8. No Data Sharing or Selling

We DO NOT sell, rent, or share your personal data with:

  • Sponsors or advertisers
  • Third-party platforms
  • Data brokers
  • Marketing companies

Public leaderboard data (team names, IGNs, scores) is visible to all users for competitive transparency only. No private data is ever shared.

9. Cheat Detection & Investigation Process

Fair play protection process:

  1. Suspicious match performance detected by admin review
  2. Same-day email sent requesting match proof/screenshots
  3. 24-hour response window provided
  4. No response = automatic suspension of team account
  5. Ban appeals via email or helpdesk ticket with contact details
  6. Human review resolves all legitimate appeals

10. Your Rights & Data Control

As a Luso Esports player, you have these rights:

  • Access: View all your data anytime in account dashboard
  • Rectify: Update name/team/IGN every 14 days
  • Delete: Request full account deletion (email support)
  • Object: Opt-out of non-essential cookies
  • Withdraw Consent: Close account anytime

11. Legal Compliance – India

Luso Esports strictly complies with Information Technology Act, 2000 (Sections 43A, 72A) and prepares for Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.

  • Consent: Clear notice provided before data collection
  • Purpose Limitation: Data used only for stated tournament purposes
  • Security Safeguards: Supabase enterprise-grade encryption
  • Grievance Officer: Available via email 24/7

12. Data Retention Policy

We keep your data only as long as needed:

  • Active Accounts: Indefinite (for progress tracking)
  • Inactive 2+ Years: Optional deletion notification
  • Tournament Data: Multiple seasons for historical comparison
  • Cheat Investigations: 90 days post-resolution
