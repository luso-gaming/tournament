1. Information We Collect

  • Email address
  • Full name (if provided)
  • Team name and in-game name (IGN)
  • Match results (kills, points, placements)
  • Basic technical data (IP address, browser, cookies)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Manage tournaments and matches
  • Generate leaderboards and rankings
  • Provide user support and resolve disputes
  • Improve website performance and features

3. Public Leaderboard Data

To maintain transparency, the following information may be publicly visible:

  • Team name
  • In-game name (IGN)
  • Kills, points, and rankings

Personal data such as email addresses is never displayed publicly.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using trusted services. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access.

5. Age Requirement

Our platform is intended for users aged 18 years or older for paid tournaments. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Cookies

  • Used for login sessions and basic functionality
  • Used for analytics (optional)

You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase – database and authentication
  • Google Analytics – website analytics
  • YouTube – streaming (if applicable)

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Data is only used to operate the platform and improve user experience.

9. User Rights

  • Access your data
  • Update your profile information
  • Request account deletion

10. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services.
