Quick Answers

How do I get my room ID for a tournament? ▼
Room IDs are shared on the tournament card on the Join page before match time. Make sure you are registered and check the card 10–15 minutes before the match starts.
I registered but can't see my slot. What do I do? ▼
First, refresh the Join page and make sure you're logged in with the same account you registered with. If the issue persists, DM us on Instagram immediately with your team name.
How long does prize payout take? ▼
Prize payouts are processed within 24 hours of the tournament results being confirmed. Winners are contacted directly via their registered email or Instagram.
Can I report a cheater or dispute a result? ▼
Yes. Email us at lusoesports.in@gmail.com with your team name, match details, and any evidence (screenshots/videos). We review all disputes seriously. The organizer's decision is final.
My account was banned. How do I appeal? ▼
Send an appeal email to lusoesports.in@gmail.com with your registered email address and reason for appeal. We review all ban appeals within 48 hours.
