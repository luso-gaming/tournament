Grievance Redressal

We are committed to resolving every player concern fairly and transparently. Here's how to raise a grievance and what to expect.

Step 1
What can you raise a grievance about?
🏆 Tournament results or scoring
💰 Prize not received or wrong amount
🔒 Account banned or disqualified
📋 Registration or slot issues
🧾 TDS deduction or PAN concerns
⚠️ Cheating or fair-play violation
🛡️ Data privacy or account safety
💬 Any other platform concern
Step 2
Our Two-Tier Grievance Process

As required under the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Rules, 2026, Luso Esports maintains a two-tier grievance and appeal mechanism.

  • 1
    Tier 1 — Raise with Luso Esports directly

    Email your grievance to our Grievance Officer at lusoesports.in@gmail.com with subject line: "Grievance – [Your Username] – [Issue Type]". Include your tournament name, date, and a clear description of the issue.

    ⏱ Resolved within 15 days
  • 2
    Tier 2 — Appeal to Online Gaming Authority of India

    If you are not satisfied with our resolution, or if we fail to respond within 15 days, you may appeal to the Online Gaming Authority of India (MeitY) within 30 days of our decision. The Authority shall endeavour to resolve your appeal within a further 30 days.

    ⏱ Authority resolves within 30 days
  • 3
    Second Appeal — Secretary, MeitY

    A second appeal lies before the Appellate Authority (Secretary, Ministry of Electronics & IT), who shall dispose of appeals within 30 days of receipt.

    ⏱ Final appeal within 30 days

Tips
How to write an effective grievance email
✉️
Use this email template

Send your email to lusoesports.in@gmail.com with the following details:

Subject: Grievance – [Your Username] – [Issue Type]

Username: [your Luso Esports username]
Tournament Name: [e.g. BGMI Squad Tournament #12]
Date of Issue: [DD/MM/YYYY]
Description: [Clear description of your issue]
Evidence: [Screenshots, video clips if any]
Expected Resolution: [What outcome you expect]
⚡
Things that help us resolve faster
  • Include screenshots or screen recordings as evidence
  • Mention the exact tournament name and date
  • Be specific — "Hacker in my match" is better than "I have a problem"
  • One grievance per email — do not club multiple issues together
  • Check our Help & FAQ page first — many common issues are answered there
⚖️
Legal Compliance

This grievance redressal mechanism is maintained in compliance with the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming (PROG) Act, 2025 and the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Rules, 2026 (effective 1st May 2026), issued by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India. Luso Esports is committed to upholding all user rights under applicable law.
