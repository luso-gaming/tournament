Best Sensitivity Settings in BGMI (2026)

Published: April 2026

Introduction

Sensitivity settings play a crucial role in improving your gameplay in BGMI. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced player, the right sensitivity can help you control recoil, improve aim, and win more gunfights. In this guide, we will cover the best sensitivity settings for 2026 and how you can customize them according to your playstyle.

Why Sensitivity Matters

Sensitivity determines how quickly your camera and aim move. If it is too high, your aim may become unstable. If it is too low, you may not react quickly in fights. Finding the right balance is the key to becoming a pro player.

Camera Sensitivity (Free Look)

  • 3rd Person Camera: 120% – 150%
  • 1st Person Camera: 100% – 120%
  • Camera (Parachuting): 150%

These settings help you look around quickly without affecting your shooting accuracy.

Camera Sensitivity (Aiming)

  • TPP No Scope: 100% – 120%
  • FPP No Scope: 90% – 110%
  • Red Dot / Holographic: 60% – 80%
  • 2x Scope: 40% – 60%
  • 4x Scope: 25% – 35%
  • 6x Scope: 15% – 25%
  • 8x Scope: 10% – 20%

ADS Sensitivity (For Recoil Control)

  • TPP No Scope: 100%
  • FPP No Scope: 90%
  • Red Dot: 60%
  • 2x Scope: 45%
  • 4x Scope: 30%
  • 6x Scope: 20%
  • 8x Scope: 15%

Gyroscope Sensitivity (Optional but Powerful)

If you use gyroscope, it can significantly improve your recoil control and aim.

  • No Scope: 300%
  • Red Dot: 300%
  • 2x Scope: 300%
  • 4x Scope: 250%
  • 6x Scope: 100% – 150%
  • 8x Scope: 80% – 100%

Tips to Find Your Perfect Sensitivity

  • Start with these settings and adjust slowly
  • Practice daily in training ground
  • Do not copy blindly — customize for your device
  • Stick to one setting for consistency

Common Mistakes

Many players keep changing sensitivity frequently. This is a big mistake. Your muscle memory needs time to adapt. Stick with one setup and improve gradually.

Conclusion

The best sensitivity settings in BGMI depend on your device and playstyle, but the values shared above are a strong starting point for 2026. Practice regularly, stay consistent, and you will see a huge improvement in your gameplay.
