Solo vs Squad – Which is Better?

Published: April 2026

Introduction

One of the most common debates in BGMI is whether Solo or Squad gameplay is better. Both modes have their own advantages and challenges. Choosing the right mode depends on your goal—whether you want to improve skills, push rank, or play competitively.

What is Solo Mode?

Solo mode means playing alone against other solo players. You rely completely on your own skills, decision-making, and survival ability.

Advantages of Solo Mode

  • Improves individual skills
  • Better decision-making ability
  • No dependency on teammates
  • Good for beginners to learn the game

Disadvantages of Solo Mode

  • Less support in fights
  • Harder to survive in intense situations
  • Slower rank push compared to squad

What is Squad Mode?

Squad mode involves playing with a team of four players. Teamwork, communication, and coordination play a major role here.

Advantages of Squad Mode

  • Better chances of survival
  • Revive system helps you stay longer in the match
  • Faster rank pushing with teamwork
  • More fun and engaging gameplay

Disadvantages of Squad Mode

  • Depends on team coordination
  • Bad teammates can affect performance
  • Requires communication skills

Which Mode is Better for You?

If you want to improve your individual skills, Solo mode is the best choice. It helps you become a stronger player. On the other hand, if your goal is to play tournaments or push rank faster, Squad mode is more effective.

Pro Tip

The best approach is to play both modes. Use Solo mode for practice and Squad mode for competitive matches. This combination will help you improve faster.

Conclusion

Both Solo and Squad modes are important in BGMI. Instead of choosing one, use both strategically based on your goals. With the right balance, you can improve your gameplay and perform better in tournaments.
