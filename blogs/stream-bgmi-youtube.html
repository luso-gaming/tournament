How to Stream BGMI on YouTube (Beginner Guide 2026)

Published: April 2026

Introduction

Streaming BGMI on YouTube has become one of the most popular ways to grow in the gaming industry. Many players are turning their passion for gaming into a full-time career through live streaming. With the right setup and consistency, you can build a strong audience and even earn money online.

If you are a beginner and don’t know how to start streaming, this guide will help you step by step. From setup to going live, everything is explained in simple terms.

What You Need to Start Streaming

1. A Good Device

You need a smartphone or PC that can run BGMI smoothly while streaming. A device with at least 6GB RAM is recommended for stable performance.

2. Stable Internet Connection

A strong and stable internet connection is very important. For smooth streaming, you should have at least 10 Mbps upload speed.

3. YouTube Channel

Create a YouTube channel and complete basic setup like profile picture, banner, and channel description. This helps in building your brand.

4. Microphone

Clear audio is very important for engaging streams. You can use earphones or an external mic for better quality.

Best Apps for Streaming

1. OBS Studio (For PC)

OBS Studio is one of the most popular streaming software for PC users. It offers advanced features and full control over your stream.

2. Streamlabs

Streamlabs is beginner-friendly and provides easy setup for live streaming. It also includes alerts, overlays, and chat features.

3. Turnip

Turnip is an Indian app designed for mobile gamers. It allows you to stream BGMI directly from your phone without complicated setup.

4. YouTube Live App

You can also stream directly from the YouTube app if your channel meets the requirements. This is the simplest method for beginners.

5. PRISM Live Studio

PRISM Live Studio is one of the best streaming apps for mobile gamers. It is widely used by content creators because it provides professional streaming features directly from a smartphone.

The app allows gamers to stream BGMI and other games to platforms like YouTube and Twitch with high-quality video and audio. One of the best features of PRISM Live Studio is its easy setup and built-in overlays that make your stream look more professional.

Gamers can also add face cam, chat overlays, alerts, and different stream layouts while broadcasting. This makes the stream more engaging for viewers and helps creators grow faster.

For Android gamers who want a powerful streaming app without using a PC, PRISM Live Studio is highly recommended.

Steps to Start Streaming BGMI

Step 1: Set Up Your Channel

Make sure your YouTube channel is properly set up with branding. Add a profile picture, banner, and about section.

Step 2: Choose Streaming App

Select a streaming app based on your device. Mobile users can use Turnip, while PC users can use OBS or Streamlabs.

Step 3: Configure Settings

Set your stream quality, resolution, and bitrate according to your internet speed. Lower settings are better if your internet is not very strong.

Step 4: Go Live

Start your stream and launch BGMI. Make sure your game audio and microphone are working properly.

Step 5: Interact with Viewers

Talk to your audience, reply to chat messages, and keep your stream engaging. Interaction helps you grow faster.

Tips to Grow Your Channel

  • Stream regularly at fixed times
  • Use attractive titles and thumbnails
  • Engage with your audience
  • Play consistently and improve gameplay
  • Promote your streams on social media

How to Earn from Streaming

Once your channel grows, you can earn money through ads, super chats, memberships, and brand collaborations.

Consistency is the key. Many successful streamers started from zero and built their audience over time.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Poor audio quality
  • Irregular streaming schedule
  • Ignoring audience chat
  • Low-quality video settings

Conclusion

Streaming BGMI on YouTube is a great opportunity for gamers to build a career. With the right setup, consistency, and dedication, you can grow your channel and reach a large audience.

Start small, keep improving, and never give up. Your journey as a streamer begins today.
