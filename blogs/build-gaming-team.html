How to Build a Gaming Team (BGMI Esports Guide 2026)

Published: April 2026

Introduction

Building a strong gaming team is essential if you want to compete in tournaments and grow in esports. A good team is not just about skilled players—it is about teamwork, communication, and consistency. In this guide, you will learn how to create a powerful gaming squad from scratch.

1. Find the Right Players

Look for players who are not only skilled but also serious about improving. You can find teammates through in-game friends, Discord servers, or gaming communities.

2. Assign Roles to Each Player

Every successful team has defined roles such as:

  • IGL (In-Game Leader)
  • Assaulter
  • Sniper
  • Support Player

Assigning roles improves coordination and strategy.

3. Practice Together Regularly

Team practice is very important. Play scrims and custom matches together to build coordination and understand each other's playstyle.

4. Improve Communication

Clear communication is the backbone of any esports team. Always share enemy positions, plans, and strategies during matches.

5. Participate in Tournaments

Start playing small tournaments and gradually move to bigger ones. This helps your team gain experience and confidence.

6. Analyze Your Gameplay

After every match, review your performance. Identify mistakes and work on improving them as a team.

7. Stay Consistent and Disciplined

Success does not come overnight. Stay consistent with practice and maintain discipline within the team.

8. Build Team Chemistry

A strong bond between teammates improves performance. Trust and understanding help in making better decisions during matches.

Pro Tip

If you are building a team for tournaments, try to keep the same players for a long time. Constantly changing teammates can affect coordination.

Conclusion

Building a gaming team takes time, effort, and dedication. Focus on teamwork, communication, and consistent practice. With the right approach, your team can grow and compete at higher levels in esports.
