Common Cheating Methods & How to Avoid Bans

Published: April 2026

Introduction

Cheating in esports is one of the biggest threats to fair competition. In games like BGMI, where thousands of players compete daily, maintaining fairness is extremely important. However, some players try to gain an unfair advantage by using hacks and third-party tools.

While cheating may seem like a shortcut to success, it always leads to serious consequences. Game developers have advanced anti-cheat systems, and if a player is caught cheating, their account can be permanently banned. In this guide, we will discuss common cheating methods and how you can avoid bans by playing safely and fairly.

Common Cheating Methods

1. Aimbot

Aimbot is one of the most commonly used cheats in shooting games. It automatically locks your aim onto enemies, making it extremely easy to get kills without any real skill.

Players using aimbot can instantly hit headshots and track enemies perfectly. However, this unnatural gameplay is easy to detect by anti-cheat systems, leading to quick bans.

2. Wallhack

Wallhack allows players to see enemies through walls, buildings, and objects. This gives them a huge advantage because they always know enemy positions.

Although it may seem powerful, using wallhack destroys the competitive nature of the game and is strictly prohibited in all tournaments and ranked matches.

3. ESP (Extra Sensory Perception)

ESP hacks provide additional information such as enemy health, distance, and location. It works similar to wallhack but gives even more data to the player.

This makes the game completely unfair for others and is one of the fastest ways to get permanently banned.

4. Speed Hacks

Speed hacks increase the movement speed of a player, allowing them to run faster than normal. This can help in escaping fights or reaching zones quickly.

However, abnormal movement patterns are easily detected by the game system, resulting in instant penalties.

5. Modified Game Files

Some players use modified versions of the game to remove recoil, improve vision, or gain other advantages. These modified files are not official and are highly unsafe.

Using such files can not only lead to bans but also put your device at risk of malware.

Why Cheating is Dangerous

Cheating does not just affect your account—it also damages your reputation. In esports, trust and credibility are very important. Once you are labeled as a cheater, it becomes almost impossible to join professional teams or tournaments.

Many players lose their accounts, progress, and achievements due to cheating. All the time and effort they invested in the game gets wasted instantly.

How to Stay Safe and Avoid Bans

1. Never Use Third-Party Apps

Avoid downloading any apps or tools that claim to improve your gameplay. Most of these are illegal and can lead to permanent bans.

2. Download Games from Official Sources

Always install games from trusted platforms like the Play Store or official websites. This ensures that your game files are safe and secure.

3. Avoid Suspicious Links and Files

Many players get banned unknowingly by downloading files from unknown sources. Be careful while clicking on links shared in groups or videos.

4. Do Not Use VPN or Exploits

Using VPNs or exploiting bugs in the game can also be considered unfair practice. Always play the game as intended by the developers.

5. Keep Your Account Secure

Do not share your account details with anyone. If someone else uses cheats on your account, you can still get banned.

6. Play Fair and Improve Skills

The best way to succeed in esports is through practice and dedication. Improving your aim, strategy, and teamwork will always give better results than cheating.

Conclusion

Cheating may provide short-term benefits, but it always leads to long-term loss. Permanent bans, loss of reputation, and wasted progress are just some of the consequences.

If you truly want to grow in esports, focus on fair play and skill development. Respect the game, respect other players, and build your career the right way.
