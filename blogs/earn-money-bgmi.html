How to Earn Money from BGMI Tournaments

Published: April 2026

Introduction

BGMI has opened many opportunities for gamers in India to earn money through tournaments. Whether you are a casual player or a competitive gamer, there are multiple ways to turn your skills into income. In this guide, you will learn practical methods to earn money from BGMI tournaments and grow your esports career.

1. Official BGMI Tournaments (High Prize Pools)

BGMI also hosts official tournaments organized by Krafton with massive prize pools. Events like BGIS (Battlegrounds Mobile India Series) and BGMS (Masters Series) offer opportunities to win huge rewards and gain recognition at the national level. These tournaments are highly competitive and require strong teamwork and consistent performance.

2. WOW Creator Program

BGMI’s WOW Creator Program is a great opportunity for creative players. In this program, Krafton rewards creators for building unique maps, game modes, and engaging content. If you enjoy creating content or designing gameplay experiences, this can become a powerful income source.

3. Join Esports Teams

Professional esports teams often pay their players or share tournament winnings. If you consistently perform well, you can get noticed and receive offers from teams.

4. Play Daily Scrims

Scrims are practice matches with rewards. Many communities host scrims where top-performing teams win cash prizes. This is a great way to earn and improve at the same time.

5. Streaming and Content Creation

You can stream your gameplay on platforms like YouTube. As your audience grows, you can earn through ads, donations, and sponsorships. Many esports players build their income through content creation.

6. Hosting Your Own Tournaments

If you have a platform like Luso Esports, you can organize your own tournaments. Charge entry fees and provide prizes. This can become a consistent income source while also growing your community.

7. Sponsorships and Brand Deals

As you grow in esports, brands may approach you for promotions. This can include gaming gear companies or local sponsors. Sponsorship deals can be a major source of income.

8. Coaching and Guides

If you are highly skilled, you can teach other players and charge for coaching. Many beginners are willing to pay to improve their gameplay.

9. Participate in Paid Tournaments

Many platforms and organizers host paid BGMI tournaments with entry fees and prize pools. If you perform well, you can earn real money. Start with small entry tournaments and gradually move to bigger competitions.

Tips to Maximize Earnings

  • Play consistently and improve your skills
  • Build a strong team for better results
  • Promote yourself on social media
  • Participate in multiple tournaments

Conclusion

Earning money from BGMI tournaments is possible with dedication and smart effort. Focus on improving your gameplay, building your presence, and exploring different income sources. With time, you can turn your passion for gaming into a real career.
