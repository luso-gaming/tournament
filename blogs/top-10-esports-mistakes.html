Top 10 Mistakes New Esports Players Make

Published: April 2026

Introduction

Esports is growing rapidly, and many new players are entering competitive gaming every day. However, most beginners struggle to improve because they repeat common mistakes. Understanding and avoiding these mistakes can help you grow faster and perform better in tournaments.

1. Playing Without a Strategy

Many new players jump into matches without a plan. Professional players always have a strategy, including landing spots, rotations, and team roles.

2. Ignoring Team Communication

Communication is key in esports. Not sharing enemy positions or plans with teammates leads to confusion and unnecessary losses.

3. Taking Unnecessary Fights

Beginners often rush into fights without thinking. In tournaments, survival and positioning are more important than random kills.

4. Poor Aim and No Practice

Many players skip training mode and expect to improve just by playing matches. Regular aim practice is essential for consistent performance.

5. Changing Settings Frequently

Constantly changing sensitivity and controls can ruin your muscle memory. Stick to one setup and practice with it.

6. Lack of Game Awareness

Not paying attention to the minimap, sound cues, and enemy movement can put you at a huge disadvantage.

7. Bad Positioning

Standing in open areas or low ground makes you an easy target. Always look for cover and high ground advantage.

8. Playing Solo in Team Games

Some players try to act like solo heroes in squad matches. This often leads to early elimination and weak team performance.

9. Not Learning from Mistakes

Every match is a learning opportunity. Ignoring your mistakes prevents you from improving.

10. Lack of Patience

Success in esports takes time. Many players quit too early instead of practicing and improving consistently.

Conclusion

Avoiding these common mistakes can significantly improve your gameplay. Focus on strategy, teamwork, and consistent practice. With time and dedication, you can become a strong esports player and compete at higher levels.
